Market Brief

A personal news digest about global markets and geopolitics.

Market Brief is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer. Each morning it gathers news from public RSS feeds and from newsletters in the owner's own Gmail account, summarises them, and e-mails the owner a short brief.

It is not a public service: there are no user accounts, nothing to sign up for, and no data is shared with anyone else.
